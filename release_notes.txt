## Bug Fixes

- Token lighting now updates on the same grid step when moving away from a light source. Previously, retreating one square out of dim or bright range left the old condition applied until you took another step, because the legacy `updateToken` hook was reading stale position data on the V13 movement system.

## Changes

- Minimum Foundry compatibility raised to V13; verified through V14.
- Removed the per-token 500ms cooldown gate. The existing per-frame queue already coalesces rapid updates, and the cooldown was silently dropping the second of any two fast-back-to-back movement events.
