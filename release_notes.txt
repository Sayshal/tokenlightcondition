## Breaking Changes

- Declare a dnd5e minimum of 5.3.3, verified 6.0.0. A world on a dnd5e release older than 5.3.3 can no longer install or enable the module.

## Bug Fixes

- Darkvision tokens now read unlit squares as dim from their active vision mode, even when the token's vision toggle is off.
- The dark to dim promotion now requires a sight range above 0, so tokens with no vision range stay in dark.
