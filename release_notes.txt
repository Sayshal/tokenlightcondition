## Breaking Changes

- Remove the Chris's Premades Effect Interface integration. dnd5e always uses the module's own Dim and Dark Lighting effects.

## Bug Fixes

- Fix a Foundry v14 deprecation warning raised when a token's light level is cleared.
- Refresh light levels when a tile is rotated.
- Update light levels immediately after creating, editing or deleting walls, and after creating, deleting, moving or resizing tiles, instead of lagging one change behind.

## Changes

- Verify compatibility with dnd5e 6.1.0.
